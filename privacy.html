Privacy Policy for HICHOU RENT-CAR

HICHOU RENT-CAR respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app works and how information is handled.

Effective date: March 29, 2026

1. Information Collection

HICHOU RENT-CAR does not collect personal data for transmission to the company or to third parties through the app.

2. Local Device Storage

If a user saves a request draft, that draft information is stored locally on the same device using app storage.

  • Customer name or draft reference entered by the user
  • Selected vehicle
  • Rental dates
  • Estimate details
  • Notes related to the draft

This saved information remains on the device unless the user deletes it in the app or clears app data from device settings.

3. Accounts

The app does not require account registration or sign-in.

4. Payments

The app does not process payments inside the app.

5. Analytics and Advertising

The app does not include advertising services and does not use analytics tools for advertising or marketing tracking.

6. Sharing of Information

The app does not share saved draft information with third parties through the app.

7. Permissions and Network Use

The app is designed for offline use. It opens the phone dialer when the user chooses to call the business contact number. The app does not require dangerous permissions for its main functions.

8. User Control

  • Save request drafts locally
  • Delete saved drafts inside the app
  • Clear app data from device settings to remove locally stored information

9. Children

The app is not designed as a service specifically directed to children.
